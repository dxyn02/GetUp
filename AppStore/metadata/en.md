## Description

Change your environment when it is time to focus—not just your willpower.

Naseo uses time and location together to limit selected apps during the moments that matter. Choose a familiar place, set a radius, select the days and times, and pick the apps you want to step away from. When a rule is active, those apps are limited. They become available again when the scheduled time ends or you move outside the selected radius.

Key features

- Focus rules that combine time and location
- App selection through Screen Time
- Reusable Home, Work, and custom places
- 100m, 250m, 500m, and 1km radius options
- Multiple rules with repeating weekday schedules
- A clear view of active rules and restriction status
- Rule and place information stored on your device

Automatic restrictions require Screen Time access, Always Location access, and Precise Location. If Background App Refresh is off, restoring the latest state may be delayed.

Focus where it matters, then step outside when it is time to move on.
